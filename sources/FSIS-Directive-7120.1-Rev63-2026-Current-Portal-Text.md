# Safe and Suitable Ingredients Used in the Production of Meat, Poultry, and Egg Products — Revision 63

> FSIS directive 7120.1 · Series Type: 7000 Series: Processed Products · **Issue Date: Nov 10, 2026**
> 来源：FSIS 指令门户网页正文（Full Directive 渲染页），2026-11-12 抓取。
> Full Directive PDF：https://www.fsis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media_file/2021-09/7120.1.pdf

## 1. PURPOSE

1. This directive provides inspection program personnel (IPP) with the latest updates to the list of substances that may be used in the production of meat, poultry, and egg products.
2. The complete listing of on-line reprocessing (OLR) and off-line reprocessing (OFLR) Antimicrobial Intervention Systems and Safe and Suitable Ingredients is available at the preceding links. An additional list of acceptable food ingredients is also available in 9 CFR 424.21(c). **This Directive and its associated documents do not supersede regulations, including standards of identity (9 CFR 319 and 9 CFR 381 subpart P). IPP are to verify that establishments ensure that their use of the listed substances is compliant with all regulatory requirements.**
3. **The Labeling Requirements column in Table 1 now uses uppercase alphabetical letters instead of numerical values.** The corresponding descriptions for each letter are listed at the top of the Safe and Suitable Ingredients Table.
